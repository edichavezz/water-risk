About the tool

A free tool for looking up what public data says about water where you live, or where you're thinking of buying. Enter a place in Spain and it gathers flood, drought, reservoir, supply and water-quality records for that spot, then explains them in plain language.

Detailed results are available for Andalucía. Elsewhere in Spain you'll still get nationwide layers such as flood zones and drought status, but local supply and water-quality records may be missing.
